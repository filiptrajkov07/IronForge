Two new platforms and a 20 m turf lane are open

The strength floor grew by a third over the summer. Here is what changed.

News Marko Petrovski 5 Aug 2026 2 min read

What's new

Lifting platforms Two new 3 × 2 m platforms for deadlifts and Olympic lifts.
+2
Turf lane Twenty metres for sled pushes, carries and sprints.
20 m
Bumper plates A full new set in all four colours.
+1 set

When you can use them

Everything is open now, during normal opening hours. The platforms are first come, first served, so keep your rest short when it's busy.

“More space means less waiting, especially between 17:00 and 20:00.”

In short: two new platforms, a 20 m turf lane and new plates, all open now.
