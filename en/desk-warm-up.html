A 10-minute warm-up for people who sit all day

Hips, upper back and ankles, in the order your body needs them.

Recovery Ana Stojanovska 22 Jul 2026 4 min read

Why sitting matters

After eight hours at a desk your hips are tight, your upper back is rounded and your ankles barely move. Going straight into squats like that is how small aches start.

The routine

Hip flexor stretch Half-kneel and push your hips forward.
2 × 45 s
Cat-cow On hands and knees, round and arch your back slowly.
10×
Upper back rotations Lie on your side and open your top arm to the floor.
8 each
Ankle rocks Knee over toes, heel down.
10 each
Bodyweight squats Slow and deep, to finish.
2 × 10
“Ten minutes now saves you a week off later.”

In short: hips, back, ankles, then a few slow squats. Ten minutes, before every session.
