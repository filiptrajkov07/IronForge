Your first four weeks: a simple plan for new members

Three sessions a week, five lifts, one check-in. That is the whole plan.

Beginners Aleksandar Bozhinovski 19 Aug 2026 5 min read

Your week

Train three times a week with a rest day in between, for example Monday, Wednesday and Friday. Each session takes about an hour, warm-up included.

The five lifts

Goblet squat Legs and core.
3 × 10
Romanian deadlift Back of the legs and hips.
3 × 10
Push-ups Chest and arms. Use a bench if the floor is too hard.
3 × 8
Dumbbell row Upper back.
3 × 10
Plank Core.
3 × 30 s

The check-in

At the end of week four, book 20 minutes with a coach. We look at your technique, add weight where it's safe and plan your next month.

“Add weight only when the last two reps still look like the first two.”

In short: three days a week, the same five lifts, small steps up, and a check-in with a coach at the end.
