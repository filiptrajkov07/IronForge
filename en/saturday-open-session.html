Saturday open session: what to expect

Ninety minutes, everyone welcome, coffee after. Bring a friend for free.

Community Elena Jovanovska 10 Jul 2026 3 min read

How it works

Every Saturday at 10:00 the whole team runs one big session on the floor. You train in small groups, move between stations, and a coach is always close by.

Warm-up Together, on the turf.
15 min
Stations Strength, sleds, ropes and core, in small groups.
60 min
Cool-down Stretching, then coffee at the bar.
15 min

Who can come

Members join for free, and so does a friend you bring along. Day-pass visitors pay 200 ден. No experience needed: every station has an easier and a harder version.

“The best part is the coffee after. Everyone stays.”

In short: Saturdays at 10:00, 90 minutes, free for members and their friends.
