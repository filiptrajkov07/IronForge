FAQ

Before your first visit

How much does membership cost?

400 ден for a day, 2.200 ден a month, 5.900 ден for three months and 19.900 ден a year. There is no joining fee.

Can I try the gym with a day pass?

Yes. Just walk in, no booking needed. Lockers and showers are included.

Where are you, and when are you open?

Central Skopje, 10 minutes' walk from the Stone Bridge. Mon–Fri 06:00–23:00, Sat 08:00–21:00, Sun 09:00–18:00.

What equipment and amenities are included?

Every pass covers the strength floor, cardio, turf, lockers and showers. Sauna, mobility studio and classes come with memberships. Full list on the Amenities page.

Is the gym suitable for beginners?

Yes. Start with a free intro session and a coach will show you the floor and write your first month.

Do you offer personal training?

Yes: 1.200 ден a session, 10 sessions for 10.000 ден, or online programming for 2.500 ден a month. Meet the coaches on the Team page.

Are lockers, showers and recovery facilities available?

Lockers with digital locks and private showers come with every pass. The sauna and mobility studio come with every membership.

What should I bring?

Clean indoor shoes and a water bottle. We provide towels, chalk and belts.
