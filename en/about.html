Our story

Iron Forge Skopje was built with a simple idea: to create a space where everyone can grow stronger, both physically and mentally. What started as a vision is now a place where people of all backgrounds come together with a shared purpose: to be better than yesterday.

Our mission

To provide a modern, welcoming and motivating environment where discipline, consistency and community lead to real results.
