What we store

Only one small thing: your answer to the cookie message, so it doesn't show up on every page. We don't use analytics or advertising cookies.

Who we are

Iron Forge is run by Iron Forge DOOEL Skopje, ul. Makedonija 18, 1000 Skopje, North Macedonia (company no. 7512648). We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at privacy@ironforge.mk.

What we collect

  • Contact and visit requests: name, email, phone (optional), preferred date and your message.
  • Members: name, date of birth, contact details, membership plan, payment records and check-in times.
  • Newsletter: your email address.
  • Website use: only your answer to the cookie message, as listed in section 6.

Why we use it

To reply to your requests, run your membership, send the newsletter you asked for, and meet our legal duties, for example keeping invoices. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

  • Contact form messages: 12 months.
  • Membership records: while you are a member, plus the period required by law for accounting: 10 years.
  • Newsletter: until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can object to its use. Email privacy@ironforge.mk and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Agency of North Macedonia.
