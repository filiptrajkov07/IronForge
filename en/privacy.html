privacy & cookies

Cookie settings

What we store

Your answer to the cookie message, so it doesn't show up on every page. And only if you accept: Google Analytics cookies that count visits. We don't use advertising cookies.

Who we are

Iron Forge is run by Iron Forge DOOEL Skopje, ul. Makedonija 18, 1000 Skopje, North Macedonia (company no. 7512648). We are responsible for the personal data described here. Contact us at privacy@ironforge.mk.

What we collect

  • Contact form: your name, email, what you want help with, the coach you choose and your message. The form is sent to us through Formspree.
  • Members: name, date of birth, contact details, membership plan, payment records and check-in times.
  • Website use: your answer to the cookie message and, only if you accept, visit statistics from Google Analytics: pages viewed, device type and approximate location, as listed in section 6.

Why we use it

To reply to your messages, run your membership, see which pages of the website are useful (only if you accept analytics), and meet our legal duties, for example keeping invoices. We never sell your data.

How long we keep it

  • Contact form messages: 12 months.
  • Membership records: while you are a member, plus the period required by law for accounting: 10 years.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and you can object to its use. Email privacy@ironforge.mk and we will answer within 30 days. You can also complain to the Personal Data Protection Agency of North Macedonia.
