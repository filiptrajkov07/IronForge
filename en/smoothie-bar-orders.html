Five post-workout orders from the smoothie bar

What to order after strength, conditioning or a rest day, with prices.

Nutrition Elena Jovanovska 2 Sep 2026 3 min read

The five orders

Everything is made to order at the bar. Prices are for a regular size.

Protein shake After strength: whey, banana and oats.
180 ден
Berry smoothie After conditioning: berries, yogurt and honey.
220 ден
Green smoothie On a rest day: spinach, apple and ginger.
220 ден
Chicken and rice bowl After a long session, when you need a real meal.
290 ден
Peanut butter shake If you want to gain weight: extra calories in one cup.
200 ден

When to have it

Any time in the first hour or two after training is fine. There's no need to run to the bar the second you finish your last set.

“Eat for the session you just did, not for the one you wish you did.”

In short: protein after strength, carbs after conditioning, and a real meal after long sessions.
