Sauna after lifting: how long and how hot

Ana explains the 10–15 minute rule and when to skip the sauna entirely.

Recovery Ana Stojanovska 15 Sep 2026 4 min read

How long to stay

Ten to fifteen minutes is enough. Our cedar sauna sits at 80–90 °C, so after a hard session your body is already working to cool down. Two short rounds with a cold shower in between beat one long one.

“Leave when you still feel good, not when you can't take it any more.”

A simple routine

Drink water Half a litre before you go in.
500 ml
First round Sit on the lower bench and breathe slowly.
8 min
Cold shower Cool down from your feet up.
1 min
Second round Upper bench, if you feel fine.
5 min

When to skip it

Skip the sauna if you feel dizzy, have a cold or a fever, or haven't eaten for hours. If you have a heart condition or high blood pressure, ask your doctor first.

In short: 10–15 minutes, drink water before and after, and get out as soon as you feel unwell.
